2
First Hike — Isskogel
Tuesday, September 1
Easy–Moderate

We take the Isskogelbahn gondola (FREE!) up to 1,900m. The hard climbing is done by the lift. Three options today:

🥇 Best choice: Summit + plateau loop. 5–7km, 2.5–3.5 hours. Views of the whole Zillertal.

🥈 If tired from travel: Just the summit (2,264m). 2 hours round trip.

🥉 If feeling super strong: Continue all the way to Kreuzjoch (2,558m). 6–7 hours.

FREEGondola
5–7 kmRecommended
2.5–3.5hHiking time
We decide at the summit. If we feel great → continue. If tired → loop back. No wrong choice — lift is free, we can return.

4
The Big One — Olpererhütte
Thursday, September 3
Moderate–Hard — Two Routes

This is the #1 rated hike in the Zillertal — the iconic suspension bridge over turquoise water at 2,389m. We leave early: 6:30am from the apartment. Parking fills by 10am.

🥇 ~6 kmA — short (bridge)
🥈 13.8 kmB — long loop
4–7hHiking time
Two ways up — decide at the reservoir. A ⭐: out-and-back to the hut + suspension bridge, ~600m climb, 4–5h (the standard route). B: longer loop, ~876m, 6–7h — only if the tendon is quiet and it's clear. ⏰ Early start essential: trailhead by ~7:50am (Schlegeis gate opens 07:00 in Sep).
🧭 Schlegeis parking

☕🥮 Olpererhütte at 2,389m · 🍽️ Self-cater + sauna recovery
